What is Public Provident Fund (PPF)?

The Public Provident Fund (PPF) is one of India's most trusted long-term government-backed savings schemes. It falls under the prestigious EEE (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt) tax category, meaning your initial investment, earned interest, and ultimate maturity amount are completely exempt from Income Tax.

PPF Rules & Calculation Mechanism

  • Interest Calculation: Interest is calculated monthly on the lowest balance available in the account between the 5th and the last day of each month, but credited annually on March 31st.
  • Lock-in Period: Default maturity is 15 financial years. Post-maturity, it can be extended infinitely in blocks of 5 years with or without fresh contributions.
  • Tax Benefit: Deposits up to ₹1,50,000 per financial year qualify for deduction under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. What is the best date in a month to deposit money in PPF?

To maximize your interest payout, always deposit money on or before the 5th of any month. Deposits made after the 5th will not earn interest for that specific month.

Q2. Is PPF interest tax-free in India?

Yes, all interest accumulated in a PPF account is 100% tax-free under Section 10(11) of the Indian Income Tax Act.

Q3. Can I extend my PPF account after 15 years?

Yes, you can extend your account in blocks of 5 years indefinitely by submitting Form H at your post office or bank before completing 1 year from maturity.
