What is a Recurring Deposit (RD)?

A Recurring Deposit (RD) is a financial product offered by Indian banks and Post Office that enables individuals to deposit a fixed amount every month for a specific tenure and earn guaranteed interest returns.

How RD Interest is Calculated in India

Indian commercial banks calculate interest on Recurring Deposits using quarterly compounding formula recommended by the Indian Banks' Association (IBA):

M = P × [(1 + i)^n - 1] / [1 - (1 + i)^(-1/3)]

Where P is the monthly deposit amount, i is (Annual Rate / 400), and n is the number of quarters in the tenure.
