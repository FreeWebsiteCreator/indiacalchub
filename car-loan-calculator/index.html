How Car Loan EMI is Calculated

A car loan EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is a fixed payment made to a lender on a monthly basis to repay your auto loan over a specified tenure. The standard EMI calculation formula used is:

EMI = [P × R × (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N - 1]

Where P is the principal loan amount (On-Road Price minus Down Payment), R is monthly interest rate, and N is tenure in months.

Important Factors Before Buying a Vehicle on EMI

  • Down Payment Ratio: Paying a higher down payment (20%-30%) significantly reduces your total interest liability.
  • Loan Tenure Impact: A longer tenure (e.g., 7 years) reduces monthly EMI but increases overall interest paid compared to a 3-year or 5-year tenure.
  • Processing Fees: Most Indian banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI) charge a nominal processing fee ranging between 0.5% to 1% of the loan amount.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Can I prepay or foreclose my car loan early in India?

Yes, most banks allow prepayment or foreclosure after completing 6 to 12 monthly EMIs. However, check for prepayment penalty charges (usually 2%-5%).

Q2. What is the maximum tenure for a car loan?

In India, most major banks offer auto loan tenures ranging from 1 year up to a maximum of 7 years (84 months).

Q3. Does down payment affect my car loan approval?

Yes, offering a higher down payment lowers the lender's risk and increases your loan approval chances with better interest rate offers.
