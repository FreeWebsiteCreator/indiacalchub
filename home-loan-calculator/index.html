How Home Loan EMI is Calculated

A Home Loan EMI consists of two major components: Principal Repayment and Interest Payment. The standard formula used across banks in India (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis) is:

EMI = [P × R × (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N - 1]

Where P is the principal home loan amount, R is the monthly interest rate, and N is the tenure in months.

Tax Benefits on Home Loan in India

  • Section 80C Deduction: You can claim tax deduction up to ₹1.5 Lakh per year on the principal component repaid.
  • Section 24(b) Deduction: Deduction up to ₹2 Lakh per financial year on interest paid for a self-occupied property.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. What is the maximum tenure available for a home loan?

In India, major housing finance institutions offer home loans with maximum tenures up to 30 years.

Q2. Does increasing loan tenure lower monthly EMI?

Yes, choosing a longer tenure reduces your monthly EMI amount, but it increases the total interest liability paid over the entire tenure.

Q3. Can I make partial prepayments on a floating rate home loan?

Yes, RBI guidelines prohibit banks from levying prepayment penalties on floating-rate home loans given to individual borrowers.
