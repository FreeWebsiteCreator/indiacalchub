Why this site

Built specifically for Indian formulas and slabs

Generic calculators often use US or UK defaults. Every tool here uses Indian-specific conventions — RBI-style reducing-balance EMI, CBSE's 9.5 CGPA multiplier, current GST slabs, new-regime income tax bands and WHO Asian-specific BMI cut-offs — so the numbers actually match what you'll see from your bank, school or the tax department.

Frequently asked questions

Are the calculators on this site free to use?

Yes. Every calculator on Indian Calculator Hub — finance, student and health tools — is free to use, with no login or sign-up required.

Are these official government or bank calculators?

No. Indian Calculator Hub is an independent website. Results are estimates based on standard formulas and should be verified with your bank, the Income Tax Department, your institution or a qualified professional before relying on them for decisions.

Can I use these calculators on my phone?

Yes, every calculator is designed to work smoothly on mobile phones, tablets and desktop computers.

How accurate are the results?

Each calculator uses the standard published formula for that calculation, but real-world results may differ due to rounding, fees, rate revisions or rules specific to your bank, employer or institution.
