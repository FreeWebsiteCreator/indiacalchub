What is BMR and TDEE?

Understanding calorie expenditure is essential for managing body weight effectively:

  • Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR): The minimum number of calories your body needs to maintain basic life-sustaining functions (breathing, heart rate, cell production) while at rest.
  • Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE): The total amount of calories you burn per day, including physical activities, work, and workouts. It is calculated by multiplying BMR with an activity multiplier.

How Calorie Targets Work for Weight Goals

  • Fat Loss: Consuming 300 to 500 calories below your TDEE creates a caloric deficit, leading to safe weight loss of about 0.5 kg per week.
  • Weight Maintenance: Eating at your exact TDEE maintains your current body weight.
  • Muscle Building / Weight Gain: Consuming 300 to 500 calories above your TDEE provides the energy surplus required for muscle growth.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Which formula is used to calculate BMR?

This calculator uses the highly accurate Mifflin-St Jeor Equation, recognized globally as the gold standard for clinical and fitness assessment.

Q2. How fast should I lose weight safely?

A safe rate of weight loss recommended by health professionals is 0.5 kg to 1 kg per week, achieved through a moderate daily deficit of 500 to 750 calories.

Q3. Do macronutrients matter alongside total calories?

Yes. While total daily calories dictate weight loss or gain, balancing protein, carbohydrates, and healthy fats is vital for muscle retention and overall health.
