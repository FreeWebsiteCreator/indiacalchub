What is the National Pension System (NPS)?

The National Pension System (NPS) is a voluntary, government-backed pension scheme in India designed to provide financial security and a steady income stream post-retirement.

NPS Tax Benefits Explained

  • Section 80CCD(1): Tax deduction up to ₹1.5 Lakh under the overall 80C limit.
  • Section 80CCD(1B): Exclusive additional tax deduction up to ₹50,000 over and above the ₹1.5 Lakh 80C cap.
  • Lump Sum Exemption: Up to 60% of the corpus withdrawn at retirement (age 60) is completely exempt from income tax.

Frequently Asked Questions

The standard retirement age in NPS is 60 years. Subscribers can continue contributing up to the age of 75 if they choose to defer their withdrawal.

You can withdraw up to 60% of your accumulated corpus as a tax-free lump sum. The remaining minimum 40% must be used to purchase an annuity plan for monthly pension payouts.

An annuity is a financial contract purchased from registered Annuity Service Providers (like LIC, HDFC Life) that guarantees a regular monthly pension for life.

Partial withdrawals up to 25% of your own contributions are allowed after 3 years for specific purposes like higher education, marriage, or buying a house.
