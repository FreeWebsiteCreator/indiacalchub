Understanding Income Tax Slabs FY 2026-27

The Income Tax Department provides two regime options for individual taxpayers in India: the Old Tax Regime and the New Tax Regime. Selecting the right regime depends on your total annual deductions and investments.

Old vs New Regime Key Highlights

  • Standard Deduction: Flat ₹75,000 in New Regime and ₹50,000 in Old Regime for salaried employees.
  • Deductions & Exemptions: Old regime allows 80C, 80D, HRA, and Home Loan interest tax benefits. New regime has lower slab rates without major exemptions.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Which regime is better for salaried individuals?

If your total deductions (80C + 80D + HRA) exceed ₹3.75 Lakhs, the Old Regime usually yields higher tax savings. Otherwise, the New Regime is more beneficial.

Q2. Can I switch regimes while filing ITR?

Yes, salaried employees can choose between the Old and New Regime every financial year at the time of filing their ITR.
