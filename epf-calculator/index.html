Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What is Employee Provident Fund (EPF)?

EPF is a government-backed mandatory retirement savings scheme for salaried employees in India, managed by the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO).

2. How is the EPF contribution split between employee and employer?

The employee contributes 12% of Basic Salary + DA to EPF. The employer matches 12%, which is split as 3.67% to the EPF account and 8.33% towards the EPS (Employee Pension Scheme, capped at ₹1,250/month).

3. What is the current EPF interest rate for 2025–2026?

The EPFO sets the annual interest rate, which currently stands at 8.25% per annum. Interest is calculated on a monthly compounding basis and credited at the end of every financial year.

4. Is the EPF maturity amount completely tax-free?

Yes, EPF enjoys Exempt-Exempt-Exempt (EEE) status in India. Contributions, interest accumulated, and final maturity withdrawals are completely tax-free if withdrawn after 5 continuous years of service.

5. Can I withdraw my EPF balance before retirement at age 58?

Partial withdrawals are allowed for specific events such as buying/constructing a home, major medical treatments, higher education, or marriage after completing specified tenure requirements.

6. How does annual salary increment impact the EPF corpus?

An annual salary increment raises your basic pay, automatically increasing monthly EPF contributions. Over a 20-30 year career, this compounding effect drastically inflates your final retirement balance.
