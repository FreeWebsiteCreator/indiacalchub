Frequently Asked Questions

EPF is a government-backed mandatory retirement savings scheme for salaried employees in India, managed by the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO).
The employee contributes 12% of Basic Salary + DA to EPF. The employer matches 12%, which is split as 3.67% to EPF and 8.33% to EPS (Employee Pension Scheme, capped at ₹1,250/month).
The EPFO sets the annual interest rate, which currently stands at 8.25% per annum. Interest is calculated monthly and credited annually.
Yes, EPF enjoys Exempt-Exempt-Exempt (EEE) status. Contributions, interest, and withdrawals are fully tax-exempt after 5 years of continuous service.
Partial withdrawals are allowed for home purchase, medical emergencies, higher education, or marriage after meeting minimum tenure rules.
Salary increments automatically increase your monthly 12% contribution, accelerating your retirement savings compounding significantly.
