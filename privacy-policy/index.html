Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 1, 2026 • Compliant with Google AdSense Policies & India DPDP Act 2023

At Indian Calculator Hub (accessible from https://roaring-boba-b53db8.netlify.app/), preserving the privacy of our visitors is one of our paramount priorities. This comprehensive Privacy Policy document details the types of information collected and recorded by Indian Calculator Hub and how we utilize, protect, and process that information.

1. Client-Side Calculation Guarantee

All mathematical calculations performed on our website—including Home Loan EMIs, Mutual Fund SIP projections, Fixed Deposit amounts, GST invoice numbers, exam percentages, CGPA conversions, age metrics, and body mass index figures—occur exclusively within your local web browser using client-side JavaScript. We do not transmit, collect, or store your computational inputs, financial amounts, or date of birth on our web servers. Your financial figures remain entirely confidential and private to your device.

2. Log Files

Indian Calculator Hub adheres to industry-standard procedures regarding the usage of log files. Hosting providers (including Netlify) log visitor requests when they visit websites. Information captured in log files includes Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamps, referring/exit pages, and the number of clicks. This data is not linked to any personally identifiable information (PII) and is used exclusively for diagnosing server issues, administering the site, analyzing aggregated trends, and ensuring network security.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like most professional websites, Indian Calculator Hub utilizes 'cookies'. Cookies are small text files placed on your device to store information, including visitors' preferences and the specific pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. This data is used to optimize user experience by customizing web page content based on browser type and viewing preferences.

4. Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookies

Google is a third-party vendor on our website. Google utilizes cookies, specifically known as DART cookies, to serve advertisements to our website visitors based upon their visit to Indian Calculator Hub and other websites across the internet.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, users can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting AboutAds.info or Your Online Choices.

5. Third-Party Advertising Partners

Some advertising partners on our site may utilize cookies, web beacons, and JavaScript in their respective advertisements and links that appear on Indian Calculator Hub, which are sent directly to users' browsers. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. These technologies are employed to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see on websites that you visit.

Please note that Indian Calculator Hub has no access to or control over cookies that are utilized by third-party advertisers. We encourage you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information.

6. India Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023) Compliance

In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) of the Republic of India:

  • We only process personal data submitted voluntarily (e.g., when you fill out our Contact Us form with your name and email address) for the specific stated purpose of responding to your direct inquiry.
  • You retain the right to access, rectify, or request the deletion of any personal communications sent to us.
  • We implement appropriate technical safeguards to prevent unauthorized access or disclosure of personal contact details.

7. GDPR and CCPA / CPRA Privacy Rights

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or California, you are entitled to specific statutory data protection rights, including:

Protecting the privacy of young children using the internet is especially vital.
